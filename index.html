01 / About

Curious about data, stubborn about shipping it.

I'm an Information Technology undergraduate at Erode Sengunthar Engineering College, currently in my final year and holding an 8.87 CGPA. My interest in tech didn't start with a single "aha" moment — it built up gradually, one messy spreadsheet and one broken SQL query at a time, until I realized what I actually enjoy is the middle step: taking data nobody wants to look at and turning it into something a manager can read in ten seconds.

That pulled me toward analytics — Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel — and a one-month Data Analytics internship at Codec Technologies, where I cleaned and analyzed datasets with 5,000+ records and helped turn them into structured reports. But I didn't want to stop at analysis. I wanted to build the systems that produce and serve that data too, which is why I've spent equal time on the other side of the stack: MySQL schemas, REST APIs, and React interfaces, most recently as part of a team building a full-stack campus ERP system on Supabase.

I like problems with a clear before-and-after — a cluttered dataset that becomes a clean dashboard, a manual process that becomes an automated one. Outside of coursework, I'm usually deep in a personal project, a certification, or figuring out why a pivot table isn't doing what I told it to.
